## dart-trap

Use case: background-extraction. This is a precise transparency correction to an existing original NetHack Atlas pixel-art sprite, not a redesign. Edit target: supplied dart-trap image. Retain exactly the central short silver dart pointing left with brown shaft and four small square metal firing apertures around it. Keep the individual square apertures themselves, remove only the large blue-gray square slab behind the assembly. Preserve original pixel-art silhouette, arrangement, palette, lighting, dark outlines and subject scale in the square canvas. Use genuine alpha transparency for all background and holes between the parts. No new objects, shadows on a ground plane, text, frame, checkerboard drawing, floor, pedestal or plate. Keep the foreground recognizable and as close as possible to the supplied sprite. Square output, sprite centered at the same relative size with transparent margins.

Keep the existing diagonal bow, curved wooden limb, grip, details, taut thin bowstring, colors, outline, scale and orientation exactly as shown. The blue-green/black colored enclosed area between the curved bow and the string is unwanted studio background: REMOVE IT ENTIRELY so this area has genuine alpha transparency, continuous with the transparent background outside the bow.
