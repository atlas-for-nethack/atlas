## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original roguelike tileset design approval board for NetHack Atlas, NOT a finished production atlas or a screenshot. Create a polished landscape board with individual example tile sprites in a clearly separated left panel and a composed top-down dungeon in a larger right panel. This is original artwork, no reference images, do not reproduce any existing NetHack tileset or recognizable franchise designs. No UI chrome, no logos, no decorative outside illustration. All artwork must fit a regular square tile grid with single-cell creatures and objects, orthographic overhead terrain, no isometric perspective. Use a coherent restricted palette, clearly visible silhouettes, calm low-contrast floors so actors and items are legible. The left panel has 12 large separated samples with short labels: adventurer, dog, goblin, giant ant, potion, scroll, chest, stairs down, fountain, closed door, wall, floor. The right panel uses these same designs repeated as actual square tile sprites, forming two rectangular stone rooms connected by a short bent corridor, with black unexplored space surrounding them. Show the adventurer and dog together, a goblin and ant in the second room, potion and scroll on separate floor squares, fountain and stairs on separate squares, one chest. Floor must remain quiet. No cinematic lighting, no depth of field, no sprites straddling multiple cells, no arbitrary illustrated scenery. Include small footer text "Concept preview • not integrated". Style direction A: "LANTERN". Crisp carefully placed pixel art designed on a 32x32 base grid, displayed enlarged with hard pixel edges. Charcoal and desaturated slate blue stone, ivory and muted brass accents, moss green goblin, rust-red potion, warm brown dog. Front-readable compact adventurer figure with lantern-colored trim, a clear head-body silhouette, simple sword. Terrain remains overhead; figures are symbolic upright sprites. No glow effects. 16-bit craft quality without imitation of any specific artist. Title "LANTERN". Clean cream typography on a charcoal board.
