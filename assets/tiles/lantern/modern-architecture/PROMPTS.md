## Matched front-facing door pair

Use case: precise-object-edit. From this reference create an isolated matched pair of its NORTH-facing dungeon door sprites, converting their material into Lantern's ancient castle style. Two sprites side by side on TRUE transparent alpha background, including transparent aperture. LEFT: closed door. RIGHT: that exact door open with its oak leaf swung aside to the LEFT jamb, a narrow angled plane attached at the hinge, leaving most of the doorway a clear transparent passage. Use the exact same frame in both states. Door is full-height upright, rectangular flat lintel, viewed frontally from slightly above, visibly thick and recessed into heavy stone jambs. Preserve this reference's high-quality densely textured hand-pixelled rendering, bright small worn edges, deep rich shadows and natural semi-isometric construction. Replace the brass industrial framing with worn blue-gray medieval stone, and the metal door panel with warm dark oak vertical planks, two thin forged BLACK iron hinges, small aged bronze ring handle. No brass bands on walls, pipes, gears, barred peephole, skulls, arch, stairs or sconces. Keep the front-facing perspective, do not make a 3D diamond or rotate the doorway. Subtle warm bevels and stone grit, NOT flat clean blocks or blurry render. Same proportions as reference front door, sized to a 64px wide by83px high final tile; demonstrate detail at 4x scale. No text, no ground platform, no background, no outside shadow. Pair only.
