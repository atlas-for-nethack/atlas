# Soot & Brass terrain sources and generation

## Current production architecture

Raised walls and doors use the actual approved Soot & Brass concept pixels, not projected flat material swatches. `terrain/projected_architecture.py` preserves the directional cap, receding face, bevel, inner shadow and frame proportions. Exact source rectangles are in `terrain/architecture-crops.json`; original reference PNG hashes are in `terrain/provenance.json`. All sampling is nearest-neighbor. The earlier flat wall/door/bar construction functions were removed after visual comparison exposed their loss of depth.

The north wall has no pipes, at the user's request. Bare stone and the original brass connector alternate without changing wall depth. Source north frames are 64 by 71 at offset `[0,-7]`; south frames are 64 by 38. Source side walls are 51 by 64. Stable side-door frames retain their 51 by 108 silhouette at offset `[13,-44]` or `[0,-44]`, and only their inner leaves change on opening. Common original jambs remain fixed. Openings and bar gaps are true alpha, allowing independently supplied known ground beneath them. Front doors preserve the first concept's iron frame and leaf, with a separate open-leaf pose. Shared walls and junctions join original directional bands according to canonical topology and existing perceived-side masks.

The room floor is the exact approved Odd Company slab `[606,291,704,384]`, preserving its actual square boundary and pixel clusters. Canonical portrait cells fit complete architecture art for inventory use. Map art is supplied separately through `projected`, keyed by final canonical/variant tile ID, with image, 64-pitch offset and floor-contact depth. North/source-facing frames carry optional complete `alternates` for the original brass connector rhythm. No engine state or hidden-world inference is added.

## Fixture, trap and effect sources

The three original sheets below were generated using the built-in image tool. Their unchanged files are retained. `terrain/prepare_sources.py` extracts complete fixture/trap silhouettes across the whole sheet before cropping, keeps detached details with the correct main subject, and records each extracted file hash in `terrain/extraction.json`. Effects retain their reviewed proportional four-by-four slicing, including detached sparks. All scaling and rotation are nearest-neighbor. Source generation is not a substitute for visual acceptance in the actual app renderer.
