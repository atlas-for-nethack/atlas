Your Beginner supply chest

Look for the named, unlocked supply chest nearby. Move onto its square, then choose Loot container under At your fingertips, or find loot in Actions. Return to the chest’s square whenever you need another supply.

Magic whistle: After taking it, choose Apply (a) and select the whistle to call your existing pets on the same level. It cannot summon a new pet or bring one from another floor.

Food ration: After taking one, choose Eat (e) and select it when you need food. Samurai know their rations as gunyoki.

Healing potion: After taking it, choose Quaff (q) and select it when you need to recover health.

Explore at your own pace

Explore is NetHack’s native, non-scoring discovery mode. You begin with a wand of wishing. Use Zap to use it and request an item.

If NetHack asks Die?, choose No to keep playing. You can still choose to end the adventure. Restoring an Explore save also offers to keep the save file.

Explore adventures have their own saves and do not enter the high-score list. Normal role supplies apply, without the Beginner supply chest.

A Pauper start

You begin without items or spells and with no trained weapon or spell skills. Find equipment and develop skills as you play.

You can still have a pet; a Knight’s pony starts without a saddle. There is no Beginner supply chest or Explore wand. Death and scoring work as in Standard, and Pauper saves are kept separately.

Click a neighboring tile to move one step, including diagonally. Click a neighboring creature to attempt a normal move or attack. Hover for field notes. Scroll to explore the map. Use + and − above the map to adjust the view. NetHack’s full keyboard commands remain available.
